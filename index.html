About Us

Pebbl is built on a simple idea: every small act of kindness creates ripples. We make it easy for people to find local volunteer groups, join initiatives nearby, and turn good intentions into real action.

🎯

Our Mission

To connect willing volunteers with the communities that need them, so helping out is simple, welcoming, and open to everyone.

🌱

What We Do

We bring together local volunteer groups and showcase their initiatives, from community clean-ups to neighbourhood support drives.

🤝

Our Values

Kindness, inclusion, and teamwork. Big change starts with ordinary people showing up for each other.

Our Story

Pebbl began as a hackathon project from a team that believes a single pebble can start a wave. We saw how hard it can be to find volunteer opportunities close to home, so we built a place where people and causes can meet.

Meet the People Behind Pebbl

We are Nommination — four friends who believe a single pebble can start a wave. Pebbl was born out of a hackathon weekend and a shared frustration: wanting to help, but never quite knowing where to start. So we built the place we wished existed.
